FMS development update

This update will primarily cover the FMS.

It's been a long time since the last update and we finally have made enough progress on the FMS to present it to you!

Our main effort this month has been on the inner workings of the A220-300's FMS. We're not aiming at just making the screens pretty, we use up to date reference imagery making the FMS think like and feel like the real thing. That involves writing systems that will read the various massive navigation databases, understand the waypoint coordinates and work out things like fuel consumption and flight paths. A lot of trial and effort has been spent on how the screens behave when you're entering data (i.e. checking for errors, updating the page with your weight and balance and your active route etc).

Alongside the coding we've been spending a lot of time this month putting together our new dev team which is a massive step forward for us as a now free project. Bringing a new team of developers on board means setting up shared workspaces and splitting the work so that the workflow is as optimized as humanly possible . We now have specific people working on specific bits of the code (PFD, MFD, Synoptics, and the FMS). This new structure has already cleared the bottlenecks that were previously holding us back.

Having a full coding team is a massive improvement to our day to day working and just makes working together just that bit easier.

Our next update is aimed to be posted in the first weeks of December alongside the end of year newsletter that will be available both here and on our Discord server https://discord.gg/hv9fDUbbdx

Regards, The Nexus Simulations Team!
